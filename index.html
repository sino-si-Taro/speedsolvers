THE LANGUAGE OF CUBING

Master the Notation

Learn how to read algorithms before memorizing them.

R

Right

Turn the right face clockwise.

U

Up

Turn the top face clockwise.

F

Front

Turn the front face clockwise.

L

Left

Turn the left face clockwise.

D

Down

Turn the bottom face clockwise.

B

Back

Turn the back face clockwise.

R
Clockwise

A normal face turn, viewed directly at that face.

R'
Counterclockwise

An apostrophe means turn that face the opposite way.

R2
Half turn

The face turns 180 degrees.

M
Middle slice

The middle slice between the left and right faces.

Remember: clockwise and counterclockwise are defined as if you were looking directly at the face being turned.
